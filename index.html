Gryffindor — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 23, 2026

Gryffindor is a family chore, reward, and allowance tracker for iPhone. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we don't collect your data — it stays on your devices and in your family's own iCloud.

The one thing to know

Gryffindor has no servers of its own. We, the developers, never receive, store, or have access to anything your family enters. There is:

  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third‑party SDKs.
  • No developer-run backend — nothing is sent to us.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Everything you create lives in two places only: on your device, and — if you turn on iCloud sync — in your family's private iCloud account, which is controlled by you and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot read it.

What information the app stores

You and your family enter this information so the app can work. It is stored on your device and synced through your own iCloud to the family members you invite:

  • Profiles: each family member's name and role (parent or child), an optional profile photo, an optional profile color, and an optional birth date (used to show ages and birthday reminders).
  • Family activity: chores, rewards, goals, point balances, allowance and savings amounts, swear‑jar adjustments, household rules, achievements, and the in‑app notification feed.
  • Calendar events you create in the app, and — only if you enable it — events the app adds to your device calendar.
  • Documents and photos you attach: chore "evidence" photos, permission‑slip images or PDFs, and a parent's saved signature.
  • Free‑text you type (notes, descriptions, reasons). Please avoid entering sensitive details you don't want stored.
  • Sign in with Apple identifier: if you sign in, Apple gives the app an identifier (and, if you allow it, your name/email) used only to link you to your family. It is stored in your device's Keychain and is never sent to us.

Information we do NOT collect

  • No location. The app does not request or use your location.
  • No phone numbers, no contacts, no address book.
  • No advertising identifiers, no cross‑app tracking, no usage analytics.
  • No browsing history.

Children's privacy

Gryffindor is designed for families. A parent or guardian sets up the household and controls it. Any information about a child is entered by, and visible to, the family — it is stored in the family's own iCloud, is never sold or shared, and is never used for advertising or analytics. There are no third parties involved. Parents can edit or delete a child's information, or remove the child, at any time inside the app.

If you believe a child's information has been stored in a way you didn't intend, a parent can delete it in the app, or contact us using the details below.

Permissions the app may ask for

Each permission is optional and used only for the feature it powers:

  • Photos / Camera — to choose a profile avatar or attach a photo to a chore or permission slip.
  • Calendar — only if you choose to sync family events to your device calendar.
  • Notifications — to send chore reminders and family updates.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — only if you turn on the optional app passcode lock.

You can change any of these at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Network activity

The only times the app uses the network are:

  • iCloud sync — to keep your family's data in step across your devices, via your own iCloud account (Apple).
  • Reward links — if you paste a web link into a reward, the app fetches a preview image from the website you chose. No data about you is sent.
  • In‑app feedback — if the optional Breakpoint companion app is installed, tapping "Send Feedback" hands off to it. Only the information you choose to type there is sent; the button is hidden if that app isn't installed.

How your data is stored and protected

Your data is stored on your device and in your iCloud account, where it is protected by Apple's encryption and your device passcode. The app also offers an optional passcode / Face ID lock for an extra layer of protection.

Keeping, exporting, and deleting your data

Because the data is yours and lives in your own storage:

  • Export: you can export your family's data from the app.
  • Delete: delete individual items in the app, remove a family member, leave a family, or delete the app to remove its local data. Removing data from iCloud follows Apple's standard iCloud controls.
  • Sign out clears the Sign in with Apple identifier from your device.
